Ordering Dashboard Solutions

Project information

  • Category: Web Application & Management
  • Frontend: Next.js & Material UI
  • Backend: Node.js & NestJS
  • Database: PostgreSQL

Ordering Dashboard Solutions is a comprehensive restaurant management system designed to streamline daily operations, manage orders in real time, and centralize restaurant workflows.

  • Table management and dynamic floor status tracking.
  • Real-time order processing and kitchen workflow routing.
  • Menu catalog and dynamic pricing management.
  • Inventory control and stock consumption monitoring.
  • Centralized billing, invoicing, and revenue reporting.
